Pan-India Coverage

A Network Built for India's Freight Corridors.

Fortune Logistics has built its network around India's most active freight corridors — starting from our headquarters in Pune, one of India's most strategically located logistics hubs. From Pune, our lanes radiate outward across the western, northern, southern, and eastern belts of the country.

Every lane we operate is backed by owned vehicles, fixed departure schedules, and GPS tracking from origin to destination. We don't broker out lanes — we run them ourselves, which means consistent service standards on every route.
