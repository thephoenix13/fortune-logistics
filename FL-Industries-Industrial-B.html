About This Sector

Industrial Freight Is Not Standard Logistics.

Heavy equipment, over-dimensional cargo, and infrastructure project freight can't be handled with a standard trailer and a standard driver. These loads require specialist planning, specific trailer configurations, regulatory compliance, and coordination across multiple authorities — well before the truck even moves.

Fortune Logistics has built operational capability specifically for industrial and project cargo. We survey routes, acquire permits, arrange police escorts where required, deploy the right trailer configuration, and manage every element of the move from loading to delivery confirmation — so your project timeline doesn't slip because of logistics.

Challenges We Solve

The Three Hardest Parts of Industrial Logistics.

We've built operational capability around the exact pain points that make industrial freight difficult — so you don't have to manage them yourself.

Route Complexity
ODC loads can't take every highway. We conduct pre-move route surveys, identify height and weight restrictions, and plan the safest, most efficient path from origin to destination.
Permits & Escort Coordination
ODC movements require state transport permits and, in many cases, police escort vehicles. Fortune Logistics manages the entire regulatory process — so you don't have to chase paperwork across multiple offices.
Specialized Equipment
Flatbed, lowbed, or extendable trailer — the right configuration matters. Our specialized fleet is maintained and operated by trained drivers experienced in heavy and over-dimensional load handling.
