Every vehicle Fortune puts on your lane is owned by us. No brokers, no sub-contractors. Ownership means we control quality and accountability.
02
GPS Tracking on Every Shipment
Real-time visibility from pickup to delivery. Every truck in our fleet is GPS-enabled and monitored on every movement.
03

Every driver is background-verified and trained in defensive driving. We maintain a stable, trained workforce — not platform operators.
