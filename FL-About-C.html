Our Story

A Freight Company That Owns What It Promises.

Fortune Logistics was founded in 2022 in Pune with a single conviction: that reliable logistics is only possible when you own the chain of accountability — starting with the truck.

From day one, we made the deliberate decision to build an owned fleet instead of brokering capacity. Every vehicle in our network belongs to Fortune Logistics. That means we set the standards for maintenance, driver training, loading protocols, and tracking — not a third-party operator.

"We built Fortune Logistics because we believed accountability in logistics starts with owning the truck."

In 2023, we achieved ISO 9001-2015 certification — formal recognition that our quality management systems meet international standards. Today we serve 500+ enterprise clients across automotive, FMCG, pharmaceutical, and industrial sectors, covering 50+ cities across all 28 states of India.

We are not the largest logistics company in India. We are, by design, the most accountable one on the lanes we operate.
