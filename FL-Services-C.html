Service 01 — Full Truck Load

When the Entire Truck is Yours.

FTL assigns a dedicated vehicle solely to your cargo. No consolidation, no co-loading, no waiting for another client's freight. Direct routing from origin to destination — with a driver, a GPS-tracked truck, and a committed timeline.

01
Dedicated vehicle — no co-loading
Your cargo is the only cargo. No sharing, no stops for other pickups.
02
Direct origin-to-destination routing
No intermediate stops. Fastest possible transit on your lane.
03
10T, 20T, 32T, 40T configurations
Multiple vehicle sizes to match your cargo volume precisely.
04
GPS tracked + Digital POD on delivery
Live location visibility and digitally signed proof of delivery.

Service 02 — Part Truck Load

Pay for the Space You Need.

PTL consolidates your shipment with other cargo on the same vehicle — reducing cost without compromising tracking or delivery speed. Every consignment is tracked individually end-to-end.

01
Consolidated freight — economical for smaller loads
Share vehicle space, not tracking or accountability.
02
Independent tracking per consignment
Your shipment has its own tracking — no dependency on other loads.
03
Regular scheduled departures on key lanes
Predictable departure schedules on high-frequency corridors.
04
WhatsApp milestones + Digital POD
Status updates dispatched, in-transit, and delivered — on WhatsApp.

Service 03 — ODC / Project Cargo

When the Load is Larger Than the Road Expects.

Over-dimensional cargo requires specialist planning, not just a bigger truck. Fortune Logistics surveys the route, acquires permits, arranges escorts, and coordinates every element of the move from loading to delivery confirmation.

01
Flatbed, lowbed, extendable trailer fleet
Specialized vehicles matched to your cargo's dimensions and weight.
02
Pre-move route survey and permit acquisition
Route assessed for clearances; all statutory permits secured before movement.
03
Police escort coordination as required
Managed liaison with local authorities for safe passage of over-limit loads.
04
Full project management — loading to delivery
One point of accountability from rigging at origin to sign-off at destination.
