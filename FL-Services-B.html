Service 01

Full Truck Load (FTL)

A dedicated vehicle, direct routing, single client's cargo. FTL is the right choice for high-volume, time-critical freight that can't afford a consolidation delay.

Dedicated vehicle — no co-loading
Direct origin-to-destination routing
10T to 40T configurations available
GPS tracked, Digital POD on delivery
Request FTL Quote
Service 02

Part Truck Load (PTL)

Share the truck, not the accountability. PTL consolidates freight from multiple clients — you pay for the space you use, and we track your consignment independently throughout.

Consolidated freight — economical for smaller loads
Full end-to-end tracking per consignment
Regular lane departures — no long waits
WhatsApp milestone updates + Digital POD
Request PTL Quote
Service 03

ODC / Project Cargo

Over-dimensional loads demand specialist handling. We manage the entire move — route survey, permits, specialized trailers, police escort, and coordinated delivery to your site.

Flatbed, lowbed, extendable trailer fleet
Pre-move route survey and permit acquisition
Police escort arrangements as required
Full project coordination, loading to delivery
Discuss Your ODC Move

Packaging Solutions

Wooden crating for heavy industrial equipment
Custom foam-lined packaging for sensitive cargo
Pallet wrapping and securing to standards
On-site packaging at origin, unpacking at destination
