Overview

Pharma Freight Needs More Than a Truck.

Pharmaceutical logistics is unlike any other category. Your cargo is regulated, temperature-sensitive, and documentation-dependent. A missed handoff signature or an undocumented temperature excursion can invalidate a shipment — and your compliance record.

Fortune Logistics moves pharma cargo with GDP-aligned processes: documented handoffs at every stage, cold-chain awareness, and digital proof of delivery that satisfies audit requirements. Every shipment is GPS-tracked, and your account manager is available round the clock.
