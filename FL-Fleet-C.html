Owned vs Brokered

Ownership Changes Everything.

Most logistics companies don't own their trucks. They're brokers — matching your freight to whoever's available that day. Fortune Logistics operates differently: every vehicle in our fleet is owned, insured, and operated by us. That's not just a talking point — it changes what we can guarantee.

When we own the truck, we control the driver, the maintenance schedule, the GPS installation, and the loading protocol. When something goes wrong, there's no middleman to blame. The accountability is ours, end to end.

"We built this fleet because we believed accountability in logistics starts with owning the truck."

ODC & Project Cargo

Specialized Trailers for Non-Standard Loads.

Over-dimensional cargo requires more than a bigger truck. Fortune Logistics operates a fleet of specialized trailers for abnormal loads — with full route survey and permit management included.

01
Flatbed Trailer
Open-deck platform for oversized cargo — machinery, steel structures, construction materials, and industrial equipment that can't be enclosed.
02
Lowbed / Well Bed Trailer
Ultra-low deck height for extra-tall cargo — transformers, heavy plant equipment, and machinery where vertical clearance is the constraint.
03
Extendable Trailer
Adjustable-length platform for long cargo — pipes, girders, wind turbine blades, and other items that exceed standard trailer dimensions.
