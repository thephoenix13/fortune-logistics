Who We Serve

India's Automotive Supply Chain Runs on Precision.

The automotive sector operates on JIT (Just-In-Time) schedules where a delayed parts consignment doesn't just slow things down — it halts the line. Fortune Logistics is built for this pressure. We run dedicated automotive lanes across Pune, Nashik, Chakan, and Mumbai with dispatch schedules aligned to your production timetable, not a generic carrier's calendar.

Our drivers are briefed on automotive cargo handling protocols — component-specific securing, protective loading, and transit documentation that matches your inbound logistics requirements. From Tier-1 OEM suppliers to Tier-2 component manufacturers, Fortune Logistics has the fleet, the lane knowledge, and the commitment to move your parts on time, every time.
