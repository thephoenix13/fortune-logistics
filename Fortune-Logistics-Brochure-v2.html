About Us
Who we are

Built around a simple problem.

Fortune Logistics started with a frustration most shippers know well: freight that should take 36 hours was routinely taking three days. We built our model around fixing that — and kept everything else simple.

Founded in 2022 and headquartered in Khed, Pune, we run full-service transportation and returnable packaging operations across India. Our fleet is 100% containerized and GPS-tracked. We assign two drivers to every long-haul truck so cargo keeps moving through the night without forcing anyone to drive past their limit.

Today we move over 5,000 metric tonnes of cargo every day, 365 days a year — for clients in automotive, FMCG, pharma, industrial, and infrastructure sectors across the country.

How it works

The idea came from our driver welfare program. The logistics industry shortens lead times by pushing drivers harder. We went the other way.

We assign two drivers to every long-haul truck. One drives, one rests. The truck does not stop — which means your freight doesn't either. Delivery in under 36 hours is not a premium tier. It is what we do by default on applicable routes.

The name is not marketing. It is what happens when you stop asking one driver to do a two-driver job.
